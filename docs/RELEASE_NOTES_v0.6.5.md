# TXTM8 Keyboard v0.6.5

An Android keyboard for the old and more efficient way of typing on a phone: a number pad with T9-style predictive text.

This is the first release with proper notes, so instead of a list of changes, this is everything the keyboard does as of v0.6.5.

**Download:** `TXTM8-Keyboard-v0.6.5.apk`
**Needs:** Android 8.0 (API 26) or later.

## Typing on the number pad

- **Predictive mode.** Press each number once per letter. `4663` gives GOOD, HOME, GONE and the rest. The best guess appears in the text, the others in the strip above the keys.
- **Accept and cycle.** Space (`0`) accepts the highlighted word. `*` steps through the guesses, or tap one directly.
- **Multi-tap.** Hold `#` to switch modes. The label under `#` shows which one you're in. Press a number repeatedly to cycle through its letters, just like the old phones.
- **Multi-tap word strip.** The word you've typed so far shows in the suggestion strip from the first letter. Tap it to add it to your dictionary.
- **Digits.** Hold a number key to type its digit, then keep tapping to reach its letters. Number and phone fields type digits straight away.
- **Capitals.** Tap `#` to cycle lower case, a capital for the next letter, and caps lock. The letters on the keys change to show which is next.
- **Punctuation.** Tap `1` repeatedly to cycle through punctuation. Hold `1` and keep holding to open the symbols page, the same one `*` opens.
- **Start in either mode.** Choose whether the number pad opens in predictive or multi-tap.

## Suggestions and learning

- A 40,000-word English dictionary, ranked by how common each word is.
- Words you choose more often move up the list.
- Next-word suggestions, learned from which words follow which.
- Hold a suggestion to remove it. Hold a next-word suggestion to forget that pairing.
- Backspace straight after a word re-opens it with its suggestions. So does tapping a word you've already typed.
- Backspace always removes one character at a time.
- Contractions fixed when typing letter by letter: `dont` becomes `don't`, `im` becomes `I'm`.
- `I`, `I'm`, `I'll`, `I've` and `I'd` always get a capital I.
- Sentences start with a capital.
- Two quick taps on space after a word give a full stop and a space.
- Tapping a suggestion adds a space. Punctuation or a closing bracket straight afterwards takes it back.
- Everything is saved on your phone, nothing is learned in password fields, and you can clear it all from Settings.

## Editing gestures

- Slide along space (or `0`) to move the cursor.
- With text selected, the same slide adjusts either end of the selection.
- Slide left from backspace to select backwards, then let go to delete.
- Flick quickly left across backspace to delete the whole previous word.
- Tap the clipboard key to paste. Hold it and let go to copy the selection.
- Hold the clipboard key and slide to select from the cursor.
- Slide gestures can be switched off or set to slow, normal or fast.

## QWERTY and emoji

- Tap the keyboard icon for a full QWERTY keyboard with a numbers and symbols page. Shift, space, enter, backspace, paste and the same slide gestures are all there.
- Default to QWERTY, so the keyboard opens there instead of on the number pad.
- An optional row of digits above the letters.
- Swap Enter and Backspace, with a separate switch for the number pad, QWERTY and the emoji page.
- Tap `;)` on either symbol page for the emoji page: emoji by section, then old-school text smileys and kaomoji.
- A shortcut to Settings on the symbol pages.
- Password fields use QWERTY with learning switched off.

## Settings

Opened with the cog at the top right of the main screen, with shortcut chips to each section.

- **Layout:** Default to QWERTY, number row, swap Enter and Backspace.
- **Typing:** Next-word suggestions, space after a suggestion, capitalise sentences, double-space full stop, capital I, fix contractions, re-open on backspace, re-open on tap, start mode, multi-tap timeout (500, 800 or 1200 ms).
- **Gestures:** Slide gestures on or off, slide speed.
- **Feedback:** Vibration and key click, each on or off.
- **Your words:** Learn new words on or off, clear learned words.
- **More:** The About page with a full feature guide, and Reset settings.

The main screen shows the setup steps first, and the theme sections appear once both are done.

## Privacy

There is no internet permission. Everything the keyboard learns stays on your phone.

## Known limitations

- English only, with a single dictionary.
- Names, slang and newer words may be missing. Add them once with multi-tap or QWERTY.
- QWERTY has no typo correction and no accented letters.
- Learned words can't be backed up or restored, and uninstalling loses them.
- The emoji page has no search, recents or skin tones.
- Builds aren't signed with a fixed key yet, so v0.6.5 won't install over a previous version. Uninstall the old one first, which also clears what it has learned.

The full list is in the README.

## Setup

1. Install the APK. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser or files app.
2. Open TXTM8 Keyboard.
3. Press **Enable the keyboard** and enable it in Android's keyboard settings.
4. Return to TXTM8 and press **Switch to TXTM8 Keyboard**.
5. Select TXTM8 Keyboard from the keyboard picker.
